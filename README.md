This is a simple version of the Blackjack card game. You play against the computer.

__________________________________________________________

random module: to pick a random card from the deck

list : to store player and computer cards

while loop: to let player draw more cards

__________________________________________________________

How the game works
Start of the game
The program asks if you want to play. If you say "yes" the game starts. Both you and the computer get 2 cards.

Your turn
You see your cards and your score. You also see the computer's first card.
Then you decide: do you want another card?

If you say "yes", you get one more card.

If you say "no", your turn ends.

When your turn ends
The game checks if:

You have 21 points (Blackjack)

The computer has 21 points

You have more than 21 points (you lose)

Computer's turn
The computer keeps taking new cards until its score is 17 or more.
If the computer goes over 21, it loses.

Compare scores
The program compares your score and the computer's score, then shows:

Who wins

If there is a Blackjack

If someone went over 21

Play again
After one game ends, the program asks if you want to play again.
If you say "yes", the game runs again. If you say "no", it stops.

Special rule
If you get an Ace (value 11) and your total score is more than 21, the Ace changes to 1. This helps you stay in the game
